About

Infinite Dungeon is an online, procedurally generated choose-your-own-adventure game. Every choice you make creates a new branch in an ever-growing tree that is shared across all players.

How to Play

You are presented with a scene and a set of options. Choose one to advance the story. If the option you choose has been explored before by another player, you will see the existing outcome. If you are the first to pick an option, a new scene and options will automatically be generated, expanding the dungeon for everyone who plays after you.

  • Blue buttons are uncharted paths.
  • Green buttons have been explored before by other players.

Progress

Your position is saved automatically. If you leave and return, you will resume from where you left off. If you create an account, your progress is stored on the server and persists across devices.
